# Rules

1. Use your own computer and development environment. Linux, Windows and macOS are all allowed.
2. CPU-only and accelerated solutions are both allowed.
3. AI coding assistants are allowed.
4. Pretrained models and open-source libraries are allowed, subject to their licenses.
5. Do not use confidential TSCI/GTS25/NOP source code that is not included in this public repository.
6. Do not include secrets, API keys, credentials, private datasets, customer footage or proprietary third-party material.
7. You must disclose major external libraries/models and their licenses in your submission.
8. Your submitted solution must be reproducible by the interview team from your instructions.
9. You must be able to explain and modify your own solution during a live review.
10. Do not submit your completed candidate solution to this public starter repository. Use a private repository or private delivery method.

## AI-use disclosure

In `REPORT.md`, briefly state which AI tools you used and how. There is no penalty for using AI well. The interview evaluates engineering outcome and understanding.

## Evaluation integrity

The interview team may use unseen footage, altered geometry, different resolutions/frame rates and a surprise change request. Hard-coded answers or footage-specific logic will score poorly.
